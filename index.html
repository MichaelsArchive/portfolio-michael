About Me

Say Hello

Hello I’m Michael !
A second-year Communication & Multimedia Design student based in Breda, exploring the space between editorial design, digital interfaces and technology. I’m interested in how visual language can shape the way people read, navigate and experience information.

I’m fascinated by creating experiences, atmospheres and worlds through design, and by how typography, imagery, interaction and technology can work together to make them feel alive. My ambition is to explore how editorial thinking can translate into digital spaces and become more interactive, personal and engaging.

StudyBuddy - UX Design & Interaction Design

YEAR: 2026 · CLIENT: CMD Breda · DISCIPLINE: UX Design & Interaction Design · TOOLS: Visual Studio Code, ChatGPT API Key

StudyBuddy is an AI-powered study assistant designed to help students learn more effectively and feel better prepared for exams. By combining tools such as a live chatbot, quiz generator, study planner and concept explainer, the platform brings different parts of the learning process together in one place. The goal is to make studying more personal, structured and less stressful.

Additional Visuals

Process & Reflection

The process focused on translating students learning needs into a clear and accessible digital platform. I explored how different AI-powered features could work together within one interface, focusing on navigation, usability and a consistent user experience. Special attention went to making the different tools easy to understand and accessible without overwhelming the user.

The final result is an AI-powered study platform that brings multiple learning tools together in one environment. This project allowed me to explore digital product design and how AI can support everyday tasks. I gained a better understanding of how functionality, usability and visual design work together to create a useful digital experience.

Scent Orchestra - Concept & Experience Design

YEAR: 2026 · CLIENT: Scentronix · DISCIPLINE: Collective · TOOLS: Physical Items, AI

Scent Orchestra is a concept that brings people together through scent and shared experiences. Based on Tuckman's stages of group development — Forming, Storming, Norming and Performing — participants move through a series of interactions, gradually creating a shared fragrance that represents their group identity. The experience explores how scent, collaboration and interaction can turn a group into something personal and recognisable.

Process & Reflection

The process focused on translating Tuckman's group development model into a tangible and interactive experience. I explored how each stage could be represented through activities, sensory elements and moments of interaction. By connecting the four stages, I developed a sequence of experiences that guides participants towards creating their own shared fragrance.

The final concept transforms group development into a shared sensory ritual, resulting in a fragrance unique to each group. This project allowed me to explore how interaction and sensory design can bring people together and turn an abstract concept into a tangible experience.
